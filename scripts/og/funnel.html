A year on the apps, for single women 22-35

Most aren't really searching. Of the ones who are, three in four lose months of dating to a man who was never going to commit, and a fifth to two-fifths pair off.

Single women: ACS (the Dating Calculator's population). App use: Pew 2022, 25% of adults under 30 used an app in the past year, about 38% of single women. Actually dating: NSFG 2022-23, 7.2% of single women 18-35 had sex with someone they met online, about 20% of users once the model's date-to-sex rate is applied. Casual men: model, her two months-long dating slots a year. Paired off: census first-marriage rates for women 24-37, times the share of couples who met online (39% in 2017, 59% in 2020-22, HCMST), times the share of those through a dating app or site (54% named one; up to 82% counting the unclassified internet), less ~6% for falling couple formation (cohort marriage curves). The hatched bar is that range.
